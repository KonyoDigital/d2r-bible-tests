## THE ARCHITECTURE — why every reading is read twice

Konyo, after a defect I reported turned out not to be one: "this is exactly why LAWS and
engines are built and secondary accuracy verifiers need and are built — whatever is gapped
needs to be added and integrated ... we need it properly architectured".

THE CASE THAT PROVES IT. A register said an item sat at loc=floor. I called that a WHERE
defect and said it belonged in his stash. Rendering the frame showed RUNE GRIP / RING as a
FLOOR DROP LABEL in the Rogue Encampment — no tooltip, no stash. The reader was right and
the reviewer was wrong, and only a SECOND, DIFFERENT look settled it.

So nothing here trusts a single reading. Four independent kinds of check, and each answers a
question the others cannot:

    LAW          needs no evidence at all. The Horadric Cube is furniture whether or not
                 anything has ever seen it. tv/inventory_law.py
    EVIDENCE     needs repetition, and refuses to conclude from thin data. Wilson, not a raw
                 ratio: 3-for-3 scores 0.438, not 1.00. tv/confidence.py, six lanes
    CONTRADICTION  two engines that must agree, asserted continuously. When they part, THAT
                 is the finding — never averaged. tv/corroborate.py
    A SECOND READ  the same frame read a second way, graded on WHAT it named, WHERE it
                 placed it and HOW it read it. tv/retro_gate.py

And one rule underneath all four: an UNMEASURED thing stays unmeasured. `None` is not `0`.
"nobody looked" and "we looked and found nothing" are opposite facts, and every ledger
here keeps them apart.

## THE ENGINE — every module, what it does, and what a reader gets wrong

Grep a CONCEPT here and land on the module that owns it. That is the whole job, and it is
asserted by a gate rather than hoped for: `footprint`, `occupancy`, `names_loc`, `terror
zone`, `slot identity` and `lattice` all scored ZERO hits in this document the day before
this section existed, while every one of them was a shipped, tested behaviour.

DERIVED every render: which modules exist, and which are imported by another module.
CURATED in `tv/engine_index.json`: purpose, territory, gotcha, entry points — a sentence
about what a module MEANS cannot be re-derived from its AST. The drift between the two is
printed below rather than hidden, because a map that quietly drops a module is the exact
failure this section was written about.
